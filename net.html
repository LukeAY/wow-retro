                        Drop a Specific Item

                          Loot Ledger

                          A markdown action plan to share after the retro: achievements first, then each boss with its status and actions.

                          How to play

                          Loot Council runs a team retro like a dungeon. The facilitator creates a room (optionally giving the raid a name and date, which they can change later) and shares the code; everyone else joins on their own laptop and makes a character.

                          1. Looking for Group. Wait while the party gathers: everyone appears as they join. When the facilitator starts the raid, everyone sees Group Found with a 5-second countdown into Achievements; the facilitator can enter now or wait for more. (Single-screen mode skips this step.)
                          2. Achievements. Post what went well, and rally behind each other's wins to raise their rarity.
                          3. Scouting. Add the problems you want to talk about to the Mob List.
                          4. Threat Voting. Spread 3 threat across the topics, in secret. The most threatening become bosses.
                          5. Boss Encounters. Prepare a plan for each boss: propose tactics and rally behind the best. When the facilitator engages, the boss's HP is set from your plan. Then each tactic drops as loot; roll Need, Greed or Pass to take ownership. Every tactic someone owns damages the boss.

                          When you're done, the facilitator ends the raid and everyone sees the Raid Summary. Export the Loot Ledger to keep the action plan.

                          No laptops to go round? Use single-screen mode and pass one screen around the table.

                          Edit raid details

                          Everyone in the party sees changes straight away. Locked once you end the raid.
