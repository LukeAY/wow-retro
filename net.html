                        How to play

                        Loot Council runs a team retro like a dungeon. The facilitator creates a room and shares the code; everyone else joins on their own laptop and makes a character.

                        1. Achievements. Post what went well, and rally behind each other's wins to raise their rarity.
                        2. Scouting. Add the problems you want to talk about to the Mob List.
                        3. Threat Voting. Spread 3 threat across the topics, in secret. The most threatening become bosses.
                        4. Boss Encounters. Prepare a plan for each boss: propose tactics and rally behind the best. When the facilitator engages, the boss's HP is set from your plan. Then each tactic drops as loot; roll Need, Greed or Pass to take ownership. Every tactic someone owns damages the boss.

                        When you're done, the facilitator ends the raid and everyone sees the Raid Summary. Export the Loot Ledger to keep the action plan.

                        No laptops to go round? Use single-screen mode and pass one screen around the table.
